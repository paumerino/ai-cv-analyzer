Big Data Developer Intern - Zurich Technology Delivery Center (Barcelona)

What Can You Expect?
As a Big Data Developer Intern, you will have the opportunity to:
- Develop data applications using Spark with Scala or Python.
- Create unit and integration tests to support reliable, high-quality applications.
- Monitor and support applications running on the data ingestion platform.
- Maintain clear and up-to-date technical documentation.
- Work alongside experienced colleagues in an international technology environment.
- Build practical experience in big data development while contributing ideas to the team.

What Makes You a Great Fit?
- Active student status with a Spanish university or educational institution is mandatory.
- Interest in developing a career in technology, data or software engineering.
- Availability for a part-time internship of at least 6 months.
- Curious, enthusiastic and eager to learn.
- Tech-savvy, responsible and comfortable taking initiative.
- A collaborative team player with a positive, can-do attitude.
- Ability to communicate in English with international colleagues and managers on a daily basis.
- Experience or academic knowledge of Scala and SQL.
- Knowledge of Spark and/or Python is highly valued.
- Knowledge of Azure or AWS is desirable.
